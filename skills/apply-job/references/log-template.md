# Application file format

One file per application: `applications/NNN-<company>-<role>.md`. Fill every section; write `n/a` where a section does not apply.

```markdown
# NNN. <Company> - <Role Title>

- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- URL: <posting URL>
- ATS: Ashby | Greenhouse | Lever | Workday | other
- Location: <remote / hybrid / on-site, city>
- Salary: <range, or "not stated">
- Source: <job board / direct / referral (who) / outreach>
- Status: Drafting | Applied (YYYY-MM-DD) | Interview (stage) | Rejected (YYYY-MM-DD, reason if given) | Offer | Withdrawn
- Resume uploaded: <path to tailored PDF, or "none">

## History
<earlier applications, rejections, and contacts at this company from the pre-flight check, or "none">

## Role summary
- <3-4 bullets on what the role does and which team it sits on>

## Company notes
- <the 8-12 research bullets, short>

## Fit: XX% - Strong | Borderline | Weak
ATS resume match: XX% (if a tailored resume was made)
Employer's #1 criterion: <criterion> - <STRONG/MODERATE/WEAK>, <one-line honest read>

| Requirement | Type | Evidence from profile.md | Strength | Points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <requirement> | must / nice | <specific role or project> | STRONG | 3 |

## Form fields
Every field verbatim, in form order, with the final answer.

1. <Field label> (required, <type>, <limit>) - <answer or "user fills">
2. ...

## Final answers
### <Question verbatim> (<limit>)
<final text>

Judge: READY (round N) | flagged judge miss: <check> - <why>
Second opinion: <score, and what changed>

## Notes
- <follow-ups, interview dates, recruiter contact, outcome>
```
